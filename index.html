Quick answers

How do I add chords?
Tap the edit button (top right), then the dashed “+” pad. Or tap the wand for the Song Wizard, or open the song menu (the song's name) and choose Scan Chord Chart.
How does the pedal work?
Hold the PEDAL button while you play and every chord you release keeps sounding until the next one. Slide it while holding to move it anywhere on screen. In Sound › Hold Zone, the bottom quarter of every pad becomes a hold, for one-handed playing.
Why are some vamps missing?
Each meter offers the figures written for it. Change the meter on the bar under the pads to see that bar's vamps. A custom beat count gets the pulse-based figures only.
Where are the Roman numerals and arrows?
In the song menu, for any song that knows its key: songs from the Wizard and templates always do, and a scanned page gets its key read from the chords on it.
The sound is late over Bluetooth.
Bluetooth audio adds delay no app can remove. Wired headphones or the speaker respond tightest; Chordion shows a note when it detects a slow route.
Does Chordion record?
No. It's an instrument. To keep a take, record the room or the device screen with a recording app.
