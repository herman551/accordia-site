Chordion Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

Chordion is a chord-pad instrument for iPhone and iPad. It is built so that nothing about you or your playing ever leaves your device.

What Chordion collects

Nothing. Chordion has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection of its own. It does not collect, store, or transmit personal information, usage data, or identifiers of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your songs, sections, chords, settings, and any chord-chart photos you scan are stored only in the app's own storage on your device, and in your iCloud or iTunes device backup if you have backups turned on. They are never sent to the developer or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

When you choose Scan Chord Chart, Chordion asks for camera access to photograph a page, or lets you pick an image from your photo library. The image is read on your device to find chord symbols. If you keep the page as a playing surface, a copy is saved inside the app's storage. No image is uploaded anywhere.

Third parties

Chordion contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking code. Its sounds are an excerpt of the Fluid (R3) SoundFont by Frank Wen, used under the MIT License; the notice is shown in the app under Sound › Sounds.

Children

Chordion does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.
